# PowerChain Local Energy Programs

Canonical Solana/SVM program boundaries:

- `registry`
- `energy-batch`
- `energy-position`
- `energy-market`
- `energy-settlement`
- `charging-settlement`
- `supply-chain-proof`
- `pwrc-rewards`
- `treasury`

The application layer MUST simulate and policy-check instructions before signing.

PWRC is native on Solana. Energy positions are independently backed by verified energy and do not alter PWRC supply.
