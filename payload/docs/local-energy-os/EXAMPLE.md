# Example — Local Solar Trade

1. Solar Home A measures 14.38 kWh export.
2. Verification accepts 14.21 kWh.
3. After local consumption and battery reservation, 8.31 kWh remains available.
4. The prosumer offers 8.0 kWh at €0.17/kWh.
5. Apartment B requests 8.0 kWh with a €0.18/kWh maximum.
6. Grid policy confirms transfer capacity.
7. 8.0 kWh is reserved and committed.
8. Delivery meters reconcile 7.862 kWh.
9. Financial settlement uses the reconciled quantity.
10. The delivered position is retired.
11. An optional PWRC reward is calculated separately from energy and payment accounting.
