# P2P / Local Energy

```text
Seller Energy → Availability → Reservation → Sell Order
Buyer → Buy Order
              ↓
Grid / Market Matching
              ↓
Trade → Delivery Commitment → Physical Delivery → Reconciliation → Settlement → Retirement
```

Matching can evaluate price, grid zone, feeder, transformer, community, generation source, available export capacity, delivery interval, losses, and congestion. A matched order is not proof of delivery; metered delivery remains authoritative.
