# Asset Model

## PWRC
Native Solana Token-2022 ecosystem asset. Canonical supply authority remains on Solana.

## wPWRC
Sui bridge representation. Exactly 1:1 base-unit backing is required; `wPWRC circulating <= PWRC escrowed for Sui`.

## Energy RWA
Physically backed energy positions. Base accounting unit is Wh; kWh and MWh are denominations. Issuance requires verified Energy Batch backing and cannot exceed physical supply. Energy RWA is distinct from renewable certificates, guarantees of origin and carbon credits unless an external registry explicitly recognizes that representation.
