# Security and Invariants

- Physical energy is authoritative.
- Energy RWA active supply cannot exceed verified physical backing minus invalidation.
- PWRC is native to Solana and is not electricity.
- wPWRC is 1:1 bridge-backed on Sui and is not electricity.
- All active chain Energy RWA representations remain within canonical backing.
- Payment confirmation does not prove physical delivery.
- Battery discharge preserves renewable provenance after accounting for losses.
- Stale oracle values cannot silently authorize settlement.
- Signed telemetry still requires validation, plausibility, quality scoring, and policy.
- Agents execute only after intent, policy, spend-control, simulation, and required approval.
