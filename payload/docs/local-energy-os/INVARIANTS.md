# System Invariants

1. Physical energy is authoritative.
2. Canonical energy accounting uses integer Wh.
3. kWh and MWh are denominations, not independent energy supplies.
4. Tokenization is optional.
5. Active chain representations cannot exceed a canonical Energy Position.
6. PWRC is native on Solana and is not electricity.
7. wPWRC is 1:1 backed by bridge-accounted PWRC and is not electricity.
8. A blockchain payment is not proof of physical delivery.
9. Battery discharge preserves input provenance after accounting for losses.
10. Signed telemetry still requires validation, plausibility checks, quality scoring, and policy.
11. Agents cannot bypass policy, simulation, or spend controls.
